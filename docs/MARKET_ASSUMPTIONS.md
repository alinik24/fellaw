# FelLaw — Market Assumptions

Status: canonical, PR-00B · Updated: 2026-09-09
All figures below are either VERIFIED public measurements (labelled [VALIDATED — source]) or planning scenarios that must NOT be treated as market facts ([HYPOTHESIS/SCENARIO]).

## End-user population (market sizing only — NOT a product eligibility rule)
- Registered foreign nationals (AZR, Stichtag): ~14.07M; ~68% aged 20–59 → ~9.57M working-age. [VALIDATED — Destatis]
- Non-German residents (population-statistics measure, 2025): ~12.43M. [VALIDATED — Destatis]
- Foreign social-insurance employees (May 2025): ~5.81M. [VALIDATED — Bundesagentur für Arbeit]
- International students/doctoral candidates (WiSe 2025/26): ~420k. [VALIDATED — DAAD]
CAUTION: these categories overlap and must not be summed.

Age band 20–59 is used ONLY as a market-sizing segment here. It is not a product restriction (see ICP.md).

## Geographic concentration (GTM relevance)
NRW + Bavaria + Baden-Württemberg + Hessen + Berlin ≈ 8.95M non-German residents, ~72% of the national non-German population. [VALIDATED — Destatis]
Status: direction for pilot GTM; not yet validated by FelLaw sales data. [HYPOTHESIS]

## Demand signal
308,684 new first-instance German labour-court judgment proceedings in 2024. [VALIDATED — GENESIS-Online] Not foreigner-specific; used only as corroborating demand signal. FelLaw's own incident-frequency data does not yet exist.

## UNKNOWN — the pivotal variable
There is no reliable published figure for "postsidents receiving a FelLaw-qualifying consequential notice per year." Do not invent one as fact.
