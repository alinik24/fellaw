# FelLaw — Competitive Positioning

Status: canonical, PR-00B · Updated: 2026-09-09
Positioning claim: FelLaw = first-response infrastructure for consequential German letters/events — NOT an AI legal platform, AI lawyer, lawyer marketplace, all-in-one legal super-app, immigration platform, or Kanzlei automation suite. [REJECTED narratives]

## Where NOT to compete
| Category | Occupied by | FelLaw direction |
|---|---|---|
| General AI document/chat/agents | ChatGPT + plugins, Gemini Enterprise for Legal, Harvey, Legora | No "specialized legal LLM" pitch; chat/integrations are not a moat |
| German Kanzlei intake/automation | JUPUS (~€97/user/mo + €97 platform) | Do not sell Kanzlei automation |
| Broad consumer legal services | rightmart (200+ staff, 35 lawyers), Legalbird, advocado (375k+ customers) | Do not compete on broad-law execution or generic matching |
| Immigration/mobility | Localyze, Deel Mobility | Do not compete on visa/relocation management |
| Narrow claim automation + enforcement | CONNY, Flightright | Lesson: outcome + execution, not AI, is the product |
Focus inference (SCOUT sources; not repackaged as FelLaw market facts): consumers primarily want the problem gone, not legal AI.

## The defensible niche FelLaw targets
The gap between (a) a consequential event arriving and (b) a licensed professional accepting the matter: understanding the document, identifying the clock, and preparing the safe next step and a ready-made handoff dossier.

## Objection answers (investor-grade)
- "Why not ChatGPT?" ChatGPT is a reasoning engine; FelLaw's value is the reviewed German event/rule graph, verified matter state, correction/outcome dataset, licensed escalation network, and institutional distribution. If FelLaw does not build those, it should not exist. [HYPOTHESIS — assertion]
- "Why won't Localyze build this?" Possible. FelLaw must prove post-arrival cross-domain first response is valuable but adjacent enough that mobility providers prefer partnership; a Localyze entry is both a threat and potential acquisition/partnership evidence. [HYPOTHESIS]
- "Why not advocado/rightmart?" They monetize legal-service delivery/matching; FelLaw terminates BEFORE the lawyer starts individualized judgment and makes that handoff substantially better. Direct threat if they acquire the user earlier/cheaper. [HYPOTHESIS]
- "Is this legal advice?" Potentially, depending on automated output and operating structure → RDG review is a P0 company-architecture decision, not a footer disclaimer. [VALIDATED direction — DECISION_LOG.md]
