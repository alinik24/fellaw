# FelLaw Out of Scope

Status: approved direction, PR-00
Updated: 2026-09-09

These areas are frozen from public product scope until the first-response core
passes its safety and institutional validation gates:

- mediation;
- insurance checking;
- careers;
- law-firm directory;
- broad lawyer marketplace and generic matching;
- outcome or success-probability prediction;
- standalone self-service template catalogue;
- generalized legal Q&A;
- court-brief, counterargument, witness-questioning, or strategic drafting;
- family-law, criminal-defense, and broad immigration workflows;
- six separate case-creation funnels;
- generic urgent legal super-app features;
- payment and scheduling infrastructure;
- mobile application;
- blockchain;
- international expansion;
- agent swarm or debate-agent architecture;
- autonomous product-feature generation.

Existing code and data are not deleted by this document. They must be
unrouted, feature-gated, deprecated, or archived safely before removal. No
new consumer-facing claim may advertise these areas as available.

## Re-entry gate

A frozen area may return only when a decision record identifies:

1. a validated user/institution demand signal;
2. the legal operating model and responsible owner;
3. the persisted domain/API contract;
4. real-behavior evaluation criteria;
5. why it strengthens the first-response value loop rather than restoring
   horizontal breadth.
