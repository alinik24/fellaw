# FelLaw — Institutional Pilot Plan

Status: canonical, PR-00B · Updated: 2026-09-09
All elements are HYPOTHESIS pending a live pilot. Pilot's primary job: measure incident frequency and validation of H01–H08.

## Target pilot profile
An organization with a meaningful international population, recurring HR/administrative-support questions, no adequate internal first-response workflow, and willingness to provide professionally reviewed pilot examples without unnecessary personal-data exposure. [HYPOTHESIS H01]

## Preferred beachhead
Geographic: NRW, Bavaria, Baden-Württemberg, Hessen, Berlin (≈72% of non-German residents — MARKET_ASSUMPTIONS.md). Buyer types: employers with international workforces; universities/international offices; benefits/EAP; relocation/mobility partners. [HYPOTHESIS]

## Scope of a pilot
- Agreed supported-notice families (employment termination/adverse employment, traffic penalty, authority/residence decision/request; tenancy later).
- Bounded usage cap and reporting.
- Professional review of the golden set and outcomes.
- Escalation-path setup with a licensed/authorized partner.
- Feedback sessions.

## Pilot hypothesis test (PILOT→PASS/FAIL)
End over 1–2 quarters. Subscription/price line: bounded €7,500–15,000. Success = ≥2 paid pilots live returning real volume + acceptable safety + willingness to continue to an annual license.

## What a pilot must NOT claim
No fabricated cases, deadlines, coverage, or outcome probabilities. No "98% success". Evidence produced is real pilot data only. [VALIDATED — trust requirement]

## Exit gates for the pilot program
See INVESTMENT_GATES.md and HYPOTHESIS_LEDGER.md (kill/pivot conditions per hypothesis).
