# FelLaw — Business & Revenue Model

Status: canonical, PR-00B
Updated: 2026-09-09
Status: all monetization lines below are experimental hypotheses unless marked [VALIDATED]. No paid deployment, invoice, or contract exists yet. Do not present scenario prices as established market prices.

## Core economic thesis
The end user's need is episodic (a Kündigung or Bescheid arrives rarely). A permanent consumer subscription is a poor fit. The institution encounters the same incident class continuously across its population, so acquisition and trust transfer live at the institutional layer, not the individual layer. [HYPOTHESIS H01/H07/H08]

## Revenue lines (experiments)
1. Institutional paid pilot — bounded €7,500–€15,000 for onboarding, a defined notice taxonomy, capped usage, reporting, feedback and escalation setup. [HYPOTHESIS H08]
2. Annual organization license — ~€18k–36k base + €10–25 per processed matter, mid-market orientation. [HYPOTHESIS H08]
3. Enterprise/API — €50k–€100k+ annual, only after integrations and outcome evidence exist. [HYPOTHESIS H08]
4. Bounded licensed human first-response review — possible €99–€199 end-user price experiment; structure and fee split follow the legal operating model (RDG), not engineering invention. [HYPOTHESIS; defer until legal operating model is set]
5. Fallback B2B "Legal Incident Intake API" — sell structured document→facts→deadline-risk→dossier to insurers/assistance providers/HR/EAP/relocation/firms without a consumer brand (Fallback A). [REJECTED for now — do not hedge by building both simultaneously]

## Structure of a paid pilot (what will be tested)
Onboarding; agreed supported-notice families; capped usage; reporting; structured feedback; escalation path setup with a licensed/authorized partner. Success metric: willingness to pay + matter volume + safety of deadline/action output.

## Not part of the model today
A B2C subscription, a lawyer marketplace take-rate, referral monetization, insurance commission, or payment infrastructure. [REJECTED for first-response phase]

## Unit economics
UNKNOWN — no cohort data. The pilot's first commercial job is to measure actual incident frequency per covered population (not to inflate a TAM). See MARKET_ASSUMPTIONS.md.
